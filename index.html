How It Works

Two semesters. One path.

Your first semester is an eight-week curriculum that takes you from your first prompt to building with AI agents. Your second is spent on a research team: going deep on the tools, teaching the chapter what you found, and consulting for a real company. No technical background required.

First semester

Learn the tools

Eight weeks of hands-on instruction, from prompting to skills, agents, and Claude Code. You start from zero.
