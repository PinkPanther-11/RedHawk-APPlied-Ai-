01 About Us

A place to actually get good at this.

Most students learn AI in fragments, a video here, an assignment there. We built a structured path instead: real instruction, direct exposure to people applying AI professionally, and a live client project to close it out. No technical background required.

We'd rather graduate ten students who can genuinely do the work than a hundred who sat through slides.

04 Work With Us

The part that makes this different.

Every semester ends the same way: our members work with a real organization on a real problem, and deliver something that organization actually uses.

  • A defined scope, agreed up front
  • Teams working it across several weeks
  • A final presentation to real stakeholders

Run an organization with a problem worth solving? We work pro bono and we're always looking for the next client partner. Reach out to Aidan Schmidt, Director of Consulting & Corporate Relations.
