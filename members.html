    The Curriculum

    Eight weeks, zero to capable.

    Every new member works through the same path. It starts from nothing, and each week builds on the one before it. Open any week to see what's covered.

    1 / 8 weeks

      Second Semester

      After the curriculum, the real work starts.

      Members who finish the eight weeks move into research teams. You go deep on the tools, teach the chapter what you found, and then put it to work for a real company.

      Meets

      Mondays

      7:00 – 8:00 PM

      1. Research the models

        Members go deep on the AI models people use at work, one model per group, and keep building with Claude throughout.

        • Perplexityby Perplexity AI
        • ChatGPTby OpenAI
        • Grokby xAI
        • Claudeby Anthropic

      2. Present back to the chapter

        Each group brings its findings back and presents them, so the whole chapter learns what every model is good at.

      3. Four industry research teams

        Members work in four teams, each focused on an industry. Every team has a Research Lead, and any member can run for it.

      4. Consult for a real company

        A company comes in with a real problem. The teams consult on it and deliver an AI-driven solution.
